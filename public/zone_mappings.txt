Zone mappings

Rules:

Layer name must match the qs:layer attribute on the .svg map exactly (case does not matter).
Use - separators.
Controller must be yard or field.
Zone number must be numeric.
The line must be under the matching section header.
Case does not matter for layer/header/controller.
Blank lines are okay.
Extra description after the zone number is okay.
